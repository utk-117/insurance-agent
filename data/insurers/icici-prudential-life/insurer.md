# ICICI Prudential Life Insurance — insurer notes

## Medical tests and underwriting
- Whether tests are needed is decided case by case, based on age, gender, existing health conditions, habits
  such as smoking or alcohol, and overall health (and the cover amount chosen).
- Tests can be done at home (a medical representative collects samples) or at a nearby hospital or diagnostic
  centre, depending on location.
- Typical tests: height and weight, blood tests (complete blood count, fasting glucose, haemoglobin,
  cholesterol, HIV I and II), urine tests, including a **cotinine test in urine to check tobacco use**.
- **ICICI Prudential normally bears the cost of the tests it asks for.**
- Honest disclosure matters: medical tests record the customer's health accurately, which reduces the risk of
  a claim being rejected later for wrong information.

## Death claims
- Intimate by email **claimsupport@iciciprulife.com**, phone **1800 2660** (doorstep document pick-up within
  India), the claim intimation page on iciciprulife.com, or any branch.
- Documents for every death claim: signed claimant statement form, recent photo of the claimant, death
  certificate issued by a government authority, claimant's photo ID and address proof, PAN (or Form 60),
  cancelled cheque or bank statement, and signed payout mandate form.
- Natural death may also need: medical records, hospital documents, a treating doctor's certificate, employer
  certificate (if salaried).
- Unnatural death (accident, suicide, murder) may also need: FIR, police investigation report, post-mortem
  report, newspaper clippings if any.
- ICICI Prudential treats the claim as intimated once the mandatory documents and forms are received.
  IRDAI timelines apply: 15 days from intimation without investigation, 45 days with investigation.
