# SBI Life Insurance — insurer notes

## Medical tests and underwriting
- Whether tests are needed depends on age, sum assured, policy term and the answers in the proposal form,
  under SBI Life's underwriting guidelines.
- An SBI Life-authorised third-party provider calls to schedule the test; the customer picks a date, time and
  location from the options offered. Tests usually take about 30 minutes: medical history, height, weight,
  blood pressure, pulse, blood and urine tests, and sometimes an ECG.
- **SBI Life pays for the medical tests it asks for.**
- **Tobacco:** for its eShield online term plan, SBI Life treats someone as a non-smoker only if they have not
  smoked or used tobacco in any form (cigarettes, beedis, gutka, pan) for at least the last 5 years. Check the
  product document for the rule on other plans.
- Possible decisions: accept; accept with an extra premium (for tobacco use, health conditions or risky
  activities); postpone (rare, when a current health condition does not allow cover now); or decline.

## Death claims
- Intimate as soon as possible: call **1800 267 9090** (toll-free), visit any SBI Life branch, or write to the
  insurer with the policy number, name of the life assured, date and place of death, and claimant details.
  Claim forms can be downloaded from sbilife.co.in.
- Documents: claim form, death certificate, policy document, claimant's KYC (ID and address proof), bank
  details; for accidental death also the FIR and post-mortem report.
- IRDAI timelines apply: 15 days from intimation without investigation, 45 days with investigation.
