# HDFC Life Insurance — insurer notes

## Medical tests and underwriting
- Younger applicants choosing a lower cover amount may not need tests unless they have health conditions.
  HDFC Life says tests are almost always needed for cover of ₹50 lakh or more, or for applicants over 40.
- Tests may include a medical examination report (blood pressure, BMI, medical history and habits), blood
  and urine tests, ECG, a treadmill test (CTMT) and special tests where needed. Tele-medical checks are also used.
- HDFC Life arranges tests at nearby health centres or through home visits.
- **HDFC Life pays for tests done in India**; tests done outside India are paid by the applicant.
- Undisclosed medical history can lead to claim rejection or delays, so the customer must disclose everything.

## Death claims
- Intimate by phone **022-68446530** (Mon–Sat, 10 am–7 pm; call charges apply), WhatsApp **+91 82918 90569**,
  email **service@hdfclife.com**, online on hdfclife.com, or at any HDFC Life branch.
- Natural death: death certificate, policy document (if not in electronic form), medical records and cause of
  death certificate, claimant's ID and address proof, cancelled cheque or passbook, employer certificate
  if employed.
- Unnatural or accidental death: death certificate, FIR, post-mortem report, police documents, claimant's ID
  and address proof, bank details.
- Death abroad: death certificate from the foreign authority or Indian Embassy, visa documents, and others as needed.
- IRDAI timelines apply: 15 days from intimation without investigation, 45 days with investigation.
